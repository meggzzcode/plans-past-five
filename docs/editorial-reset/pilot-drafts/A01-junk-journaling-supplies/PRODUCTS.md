## J1. Strathmore 400 Series Toned Mixed Media Hardbound Art Journal, 8.5 x 5.5 in (landscape), tan 469-305 or gray 469-405

- **Brand / maker:** Strathmore Artist Papers. https://strathmoreartist.com/mixed-media/400-series-toned-mixed-media-hardbound-art-journal (maker page read 2026-11-19)
- **Role / direction:** Core. Tan: Letters Home, Garden Press. Gray: Sunday Blue.
- **Original description (as used in ARTICLE.md):** This is the sturdy base the whole edit stands on. Strathmore lists the paper as 300 g/m2 (184 lb) and the binding as Smyth-sewn, which it says lets pages lie flatter; it also states the paper is acid free with 30% post-consumer fiber. The toned page gives every spread a ground colour from the start. It suits someone who wants her layers to sit on something substantial. The limits: 24 sheets (48 pages by the maker's count), toned paper softens pale colours, and we have no evidence on heavy glue, so no warp promise. Chosen over lighter notebooks because glue and layers ask more of paper, and over other hardbounds because the maker page states weight and binding.
- **Internal score:** 83 (D20 U17 Q17 B9 V9 Val7 G4). Class: Approved supporting (not Flagship). Band: Considered.

J4 Strathmore softcover (80; used in A02 only to keep sets disjoint). J5 Leuchtturm1917 dotted (66, Reject; maker lists a lighter 80 gsm paper, wrong tool for glue).
