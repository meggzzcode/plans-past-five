# A01 Pinterest plan (concepts only; nothing posted, nothing created)

Destination for all pins: `/journal/junk-journaling-supplies-for-beginners`. Spec per E3 3.5: 2:3 vertical, 1000 x 1500 px; subject in the middle 60%; top 20% clear for title; bottom 15% for the wordmark. Type is added in layout (Fraunces display, DM Sans small caps, one Caveat accent of max 4 words), never rendered by an image generator. The existing junk-journal pin assets in `public/pins` are from the old plan and should be remade, not reused (E1).

Class for all four: editorial-inspirational. None shows a specific branded product. None is "product photography". Where affiliate links are reached from the pin, the description says "Affiliate links" (E3).

**AI-label note (TO VERIFY at upload time):** E3 says to use Pinterest's AI-modified / AI-generated content label where offered. This was NOT re-read against Pinterest's current policy for this draft. If pins use AI imagery, check the label setting, keep descriptions honest, and log the image in the licence ledger.

## Pin 1: the search pin

- **Image brief (2:3):** Overhead flat lay on cream: a thick toned journal page half-built, three tape strips, a pen, torn envelope scraps. Top 20% empty cream for the title. One cherry note (pen cap). Generic items, no logos.
- **Overlay copy (max 8 words):** "Junk journal supplies: what you actually need" (7 words). Caveat accent: "start small".
- **Pin title (honest, <= 100):** Junk Journal Supplies for Beginners: A Small, Pretty Starter List
- **Pin description:** One journal, one adhesive, one pen, one set of tape. A short supplies list for a first junk journal spread, with what you already own. Affiliate links. #junkjournal #junkjournaling #journalingsupplies #paperedit
- **Board:** Junk Journaling Supplies (proposed; create under owner control).

## Pin 2: the aspirational flat lay (Letters Home)

- **Image brief (2:3):** Warm, tan page, ticket-stub paper, a torn envelope corner, black and metallic tape strips (generic, no brand pattern), black pen. Golden-hour window light from the left. Subject centred in the middle 60%.
- **Overlay copy:** "The Letters Home journal" ; Caveat: "keep the stubs".
- **Pin title:** A Warm, Ticket-Stub Junk Journal Look and What to Buy
- **Pin description:** A tan journal, black ink and a little metal tape: the "Letters Home" look, one of three directions in our supplies edit. Affiliate links. #junkjournal #vintagejournal #scrapbooking #journalideas
- **Board:** Junk Journal Ideas.

## Pin 3: three directions side by side (Garden Press and Sunday Blue included)

- **Image brief (2:3):** Three stacked cream panels, each with a tiny arrangement: warm tan/black-gold; botanical green/yellow with a dried leaf; pale blue/gray. Equal weight; no text inside the frame except the overlay.
- **Overlay copy:** "Pick your look: Letters Home, Garden Press, Sunday Blue" (8 words).
- **Pin title:** Three Junk Journal Looks, One Small Shopping List
- **Pin description:** One shared core kit and three palettes to dress it: warm and postal, botanical, or quiet blue. Which would you start with? Affiliate links. #junkjournaling #washitape #journalinspiration
- **Board:** Junk Journal Ideas.
- **Caution:** direction names are not trademark-cleared; keep them as plain overlay words, not logos.

## Pin 4: the checklist pin ("already in your drawer")

- **Image brief (2:3):** Real-feeling scraps arranged in a neat grid on kraft or cream: envelope, ticket stub, receipt, tea-bag packet, book page. No faces. Text overlay is a short tick-list typeset in layout.
- **Overlay copy:** "What you already own: five paper scraps that count" (8 words; deliberately avoids "free" as a hook per E3).
- **Pin title:** What to Use in a Junk Journal From Around the House
- **Pin description:** Five scraps already in your bag or recycling bin, plus the four things worth buying. A beginner checklist. Affiliate links on the page. #junkjournal #scrapbooking #papercraft
- **Board:** Junk Journaling Supplies.

## Strategy

- Pins 1 and 4 are the search and save pins (checklist behaviour); pins 2 and 3 are aspirational. All four point at the same URL to keep authority on one page (assumption).
- Pin 3 may be the best saver because it makes a choice (assumption, UNMEASURED); no performance claim is made.
- Pinterest is an inspiration and saving channel more than a checkout (M1 cites independent 2023 survey data: 44% never buy via Pinterest). Do not write the page or pins as if Pinterest users arrive ready to buy.
- Keep pin images consistent in grade with the page hero.
- Sequence: the page publishes first, pins later; stagger over weeks (not planned here).
- **Not done:** no board created, no pin posted, no image produced. No claims about reach.
