## SERP observations (from E1 and the earlier pilot read; NOT re-verified here)

- Top results are generic technique primers (potclays.co.uk, Skillshare, Art of Education) and 10 to 30 idea lists (diyjoy, Hometalk, Mont Marte, First Day of Home, Crayola).
- Trinket dishes recur as the first beginner project.
- Few results appear to give a small ordered set of three projects with drying and finishing timing. This is the angle.
- Mont Marte and Crayola, which we quote, also rank with their own project and FAQ pages: we compete with the makers. Our edge is a single ordered plan and the honest limits box, not new technique.

## Title and meta rationale

- H1 (on page): "Air-Dry Clay for Beginners: Three Little Things to Make This Week" (E1 working title: searchable noun phrase first, voice second; "for Beginners" matches the primary query).
- Meta title (57 characters): "Air-Dry Clay for Beginners: 3 Easy Projects + What to Buy". Keeps the primary keyword at the front, signals both the how-to and the shopping layer, avoids "best".
- Meta description (134 characters): "Make a ring dish, a trinket dish and a small tray from air-dry clay: what to buy, what you already own, and how long each stage takes." Names the outcome and the timing, which are the differentiators.
- "Easy" in the meta title is an editorial word, not a claim; reviewer may prefer "Simple".
- Slug `how-to-start-air-dry-clay` kept (E1: keep). It does not contain the exact primary phrase; accepted for URL continuity.

## Schema suggestions

- **Article**: yes. Headline, description, datePublished and dateModified (from the build), image (hero, once cleared), publisher Organization. No author Person unless the owner supplies one; the article is byline-free.
- **HowTo**: schema.org permits it and the three projects have steps, supplies and total time. BUT: it needs a real image per step to be strongest, our steps have no cleared photographs, and Google's own rich-result support for HowTo has been reduced since 2023 (UNVERIFIED current status: check Google documentation). Recommendation: do not add HowTo until step photographs exist and the support status is confirmed. Total time cannot be asserted as a tested figure.
- **FAQPage**: the FAQ is real; eligibility for rich results is limited for most sites (UNVERIFIED). Optional, low value.
- **Product / Offer / AggregateRating**: NO. We show no prices, no ratings and no reviews. Do not add product markup.
- **BreadcrumbList**: yes (Journal > article).
