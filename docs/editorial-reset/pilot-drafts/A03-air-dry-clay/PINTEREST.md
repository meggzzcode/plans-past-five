# A03 PINTEREST (concepts only; nothing designed, generated, scheduled or posted)

Strategy: the article is a how-to with a shopping layer, and trinket dishes are among the most repeated beginner clay projects in what the E1 research read (Pinterest volume UNMEASURED). Pinners save things they can use later: a timeline, a supply checklist, a three-piece set. Each pin shows something saveable and honest, and sends to the same article. Four pins cover four intents: search ("air-dry clay for beginners"), supplies, timeline, and the aspirational finished set. Pins must not use a specific named product, and must not imply what a brand's clay produces. Pins that link to shopping pages say "Affiliate links" in the description (E3). Text is added in layout (Fraunces headline, DM Sans label, at most one short Caveat accent), never by an image generator.

Common specs: vertical 2:3 (1000 x 1500 px); subject in the middle 60%; top 20% clear for title; bottom 15% for the wordmark; cream ground, one cherry note, at most two tints. Destination for all: `/journal/how-to-start-air-dry-clay` (site path; final URL with UTM or without is for the owner).
