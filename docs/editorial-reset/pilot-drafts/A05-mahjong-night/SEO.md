## Title and meta rationale
- Meta title "How to Host a Mahjong Night: Table, Snacks, First Game" (54 chars): primary phrase first, three deliverables after the colon, within 60. Site suffix (brand) may push it over 60; confirm the renderer's title template and whether the suffix counts.
- On-page H1 keeps the full E1 working title (66 chars) because it states the three promises; E3 rule says the H1 may be warmer if the keyword is intact: it is.
- Meta description (143 chars) names four, timeline, table, snacks, first game. No "best", no price, no year.
- Slug how-to-host-a-mahjong-night matches the query and the old URL.

## Schema (proposal; verify against current Google guidance)
- Article (headline, description, author as Organization or named editor per policy, datePublished/dateModified, image once rights exist).
- BreadcrumbList (Home > The Journal > article).
- FAQPage: markup is allowed but Google has restricted FAQ rich results to a narrow set of sites since 2023, so expect no visible benefit; low priority, and only if the on-page FAQ is kept. (Recollection, verify.)
- HowTo: Google retired HowTo rich results; do NOT add. (Recollection, verify.)
- No Product, Offer, AggregateRating or Review schema: no verified products, prices or ratings exist.
