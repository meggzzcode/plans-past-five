## SERP observations
- Source 1 (E1, prior research, not re-run): for "best hobbies for women in their 30s": Woman & Home, Mindbodygreen, Dear Media, StyleCraze, Silk & Sonder, Skill Scouter; broad spread with no organising logic.
- Source 2 (my own check, 2026-11-19, WebSearch snippets only, I did not open the pages): "hobbies for busy women" returned Essence, Wonderbaby ("130 Mom Hobbies"), Nibble, AOL ("125 Hobbies for Women"), Best Life, Good Good Good ("89 Hobbies for Women"), Families Magazine, Cincy Mom Collective, Woman & Home, Care.com, LoveToKnow. Pattern: long numbered lists (36 to 130 items), several aimed at moms, grouped by category (creative, physical, learning, relaxing). "Hobbies to try after work low energy" returned Bustle, The Muse, The Everygirl, BetterUp, Flow Magazine, AOL, Apartment Therapy, YourTango, Medium. Pattern: low-energy lists with reading, crochet, colouring, journaling; one guide sorts by energy tier.
- What competitors do: huge lists, category grouping, often "stress relief" claims. What we do differently: 12 curated items, grouped by tonight's energy, a comparison table, a first step per item, a route into the quiz and planner, no wellbeing claims.
- Honest read: "hobbies for busy women" is crowded and mom-skewed; this page is a long-tail, brand and tool-traffic bet (E1), not a head-term win.

## Title and meta rationale
- Meta title "Hobbies for Busy Women: Pick by Energy and Budget" (55 chars) leads with the primary phrase, names two decision axes.
- Meta description (150 chars) states the number (12), the sorting logic and the solo/social axis.
- H1 kept long (E1 title) because the on-page headline can run longer than the meta title; E3 asks 50 to 65 for the title tag, which the meta title satisfies.
- Alternative H1 if the owner wants a number: "12 Hobbies for Busy Women, Sorted by How Much Energy You Have". Not chosen: a number in the H1 invites list-comparison with 100-item lists and makes the count a maintenance promise.

## Schema suggestions
- `Article` (headline, description, datePublished, author = Organization or named editor once decided, image).
- `BreadcrumbList` (Home > Journal > this).
- `ItemList` of the 12 hobbies with `url` to each hobby page. Do NOT use `Product`, `Review`, `HowTo` or `FAQPage` (no steps-as-HowTo claim; no FAQ section).
- Internal check: confirm the site's `seo()` helper supports these before relying on them.
