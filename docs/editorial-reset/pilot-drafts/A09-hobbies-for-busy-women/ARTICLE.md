## BODY

### Opening (no heading)

It is 5:40 on a Thursday. You have one evening, a little energy left, and a vague wish to make something that is yours.

This guide sorts twelve hobbies by how much you have left, not by how interesting they sound. Find your evening below, read the card, do the first step. Skip the rest until you want it.

### Pick by three questions

Most lists ask what you are interested in. On a Thursday that is the wrong question. Ask these instead, in this order.

1. **How much have I got left?** Sofa only, one lamp and a table, or enough to go somewhere or have people over.
2. **Do I want company?** Some of these need a second person. Some are better with nobody.
3. **What am I happy to spend to find out?** Each card shows the rough start-up band from our hobby pages. They are approximate assumptions, not prices, and a borrowed or secondhand start is a fair way to begin.

### Nothing left: sofa-compatible hobbies

You can start in your work clothes. Each one needs a surface the size of a tray, under 45 minutes, and no decisions you would call decisions.

**1. Reading** (IMG-01)
*30 minutes · free, with a library card · solo*
Tonight: pick the shortest book you own and read before your phone gets a chance. Two-minute setup: lamp on, book where the phone usually lands, phone charging in another room. The catch: the first ten pages feel slow next to a screen. Give it ten, then decide. Already have a library card? That is the whole budget.
[Reading hobby page](/hobbies/reading) · Next: [start a reading journal](/journal/how-to-start-a-reading-journal)

**2. Junk Journaling** (IMG-02)
*45 minutes · under $25 · solo*
Tonight: empty your bag and wallet, pick three bits of paper from this week (a receipt, a ticket, a coffee sleeve), and make one page about them. Two-minute setup: an old notebook, a glue stick and scissors on a tray. The catch: the first page will look chaotic. That is the format, not a mistake.
[Junk Journaling hobby page](/hobbies/junk-journaling) · Next: [how to start junk journaling](/journal/how-to-start-junk-journaling)

**3. Embroidery** (IMG-03)
*45 minutes · under $25 · solo*
Tonight: learn one stitch, backstitch, and stitch your initial. Two-minute setup: hoop, fabric, one colour of thread and a needle in a bag by the sofa. The catch: your first stitches will wobble and the first French knot may tangle. Embroidery travels well, so a hoop in a tote bag counts as a plan.
[Embroidery hobby page](/hobbies/embroidery) · Next: [how to start embroidery](/journal/how-to-start-embroidery)

**4. Crochet** (IMG-04)
*45 minutes · under $25 · solo*
Tonight: follow one beginner video and make a single granny square. It does not have to be square. Two-minute setup: a hook, one skein of medium-weight yarn and scissors in a basket, with the video queued. The catch is honest: the first hour is fiddly and our hobby pages rate this one as needing a lesson or two, so queue a video before you sit down, not after.
[Crochet hobby page](/hobbies/crochet) · Next: [how to start crocheting](/journal/how-to-start-crocheting)

### A little left: one table, one lamp

You can set something up and put it away again. Thirty to sixty minutes, one flat surface.

**5. Watercolor** (IMG-05)
*30 minutes · under $50 · solo or with a friend*
Tonight: paint whatever is on your counter (a mug, a lemon, the flowers) in three colours only. Two-minute setup: fill the water jar before you sit down. The catch: a small number of colours keeps it calm; add a fourth only if you miss it.
[Watercolor hobby page](/hobbies/watercolor-painting) · Next: [watercolor for beginners](/journal/watercolor-painting-for-beginners)

**6. Air-Dry Clay** (IMG-06)
*About an hour · under $50 · solo or with a friend*
Tonight: roll a ball, press your thumb in, and shape a ring dish. Paint it once it has dried. Two-minute setup: a clear table and a rolling pin or a glass. The catch: this is a two-evening hobby. Drying time depends on the clay and how thick you make it, so follow the maker's instructions on the pack and paint on another night.
[Air-Dry Clay hobby page](/hobbies/air-dry-clay) · Next: [how to start air-dry clay](/journal/how-to-start-air-dry-clay)

**7. Flower Arranging** (IMG-07)
*30 minutes · under $50 · solo or with a friend*
Tonight: buy one bunch of supermarket flowers on the way home and split it into three small jars. Two-minute setup: scissors out, jars rinsed. The catch: flowers are a repeat purchase, not a one-off, so decide whether a weekly bunch fits before it becomes a habit.
[Flower Arranging hobby page](/hobbies/flower-arranging) · Next: [flower arranging as a hobby](/journal/flower-arranging-as-a-hobby)

### Enough left to go somewhere, or have people over

These take an evening or an afternoon and a second person. They are the ones that end up in the group chat.

**8. Craft and Paint Nights** (IMG-08)
*About 2.5 hours · under $50 · with friends*
Tonight, or this week: invite three friends for one easy craft (bracelets are the classic) with snacks and a table. Two-minute setup: pick the craft before you invite anyone, so the invitation names a plan. The catch: you are the host, so choose something that works at a crowded table.
[Craft Nights hobby page](/hobbies/craft-nights) · Next: [host a craft night](/journal/how-to-host-a-craft-night) or [paint night at home](/journal/how-to-host-a-paint-night-at-home)

**9. Mahjong** (IMG-09)
*2 hours · varies, can start small · with friends*
Tonight: watch one beginner tutorial together and play a practice round with the tiles face up. Two-minute setup: four people, a set (borrowing counts) and a rules card. The catch: this one has a real learning curve, so plan the first night as practice, not a game. The budget depends on the set, so ask around before buying.
[Mahjong hobby page](/hobbies/mahjong) · Next: [how to host a mahjong night](/journal/how-to-host-a-mahjong-night)

**10. Picnicking** (IMG-10)
*2 hours · under $50 · with a friend, or alone with a book*
Tonight: pack bread, fruit and cheese and claim a patch of grass on the next dry day. Two-minute setup: a blanket by the door and a bag you can carry in one hand. The catch: weather decides, so name a backup evening when you make the plan.
[Picnicking hobby page](/hobbies/picnicking) · Next: [how to plan a perfect picnic](/journal/how-to-plan-a-perfect-picnic)

**11. Pickleball** (IMG-11)
*About an hour · under $50 · with people*
This week: find a public court with open play and show up with a friend. Two-minute setup: sneakers by the door, and check your local parks page for court hours before you go. The catch: courts and open-play times vary by town, so confirm the schedule first rather than arriving to a closed gate.
[Pickleball hobby page](/hobbies/pickleball) · Next: [pickleball for beginners](/journal/pickleball-for-beginners)

**12. Tennis** (IMG-12)
*About an hour · under $50 · with a friend*
This week: take a friend to a public court and rally for thirty minutes, with no score. Two-minute setup: a racket borrowed or secondhand is a reasonable way to find out if you like it. The catch: this is the highest-energy card here, so it is a Saturday pick more than a Thursday one.
[Tennis hobby page](/hobbies/tennis) · Next: [how to start playing tennis as an adult](/journal/how-to-start-playing-tennis-as-an-adult)

### Three quick filters

If you only have one constraint, start here.

- **Thirty minutes, tonight:** Reading, Watercolor, Flower Arranging.
- **Starting for under $25, or free:** Reading, Junk Journaling, Embroidery, Crochet.
- **Better with other people:** Craft and Paint Nights, Mahjong, Picnicking, Pickleball, Tennis.

### Make your shortlist of three

Choose three cards that made you think "maybe". Not the one you think you should like. Write them on a sticky note and put it on the fridge. You are not committing; you are narrowing.

Then pick the one with the smallest first step and do only that step this week.

- **Still torn?** [Take the hobby quiz](/hobby-finder). It asks about your energy, your budget and your company and suggests hobbies to try.
- **Know your pick, need an evening for it?** [Build the evening in Make Plans](/make-plans).
- **Want more than twelve?** [Browse the full hobby directory](/hobbies) and filter by low energy, after work or under $25.
- **Save this page** with the heart so the matrix is there when Thursday comes round again.

### Closing

Pick the card with the smallest first step. Do only that step on the next evening you have an hour, even if the result is lopsided.
