# PRODUCTS: A09 Hobbies for Busy Women

**No products by design.**

Why: A09 is a traffic and discovery pillar (E1 role "Traffic / discovery pillar"; E1 says "None named inline; routes to hobby pages"). Its job is to help a reader choose and start, and the commercial value flows through the signature-experience articles (A01, A03, A05 and the rest), where products have been researched under the Amazon-first gate. Naming any product here would force a recommendation before the reader has chosen a hobby, and none of the pilot research files (A01, A03, A05 v2) is a verified or approved shortlist; every ASIN there is an unverified lead.

What the draft does mention:
- Generic, unbranded items only as a setup description ("a glue stick", "a hoop", "a blanket", "a library card"). These are not product recommendations, carry no links, and need no research record.
- Nothing from the pilot research files is named.

Consequences:
- No affiliate links, so the Amazon-required Associate statement and AffiliateNote are NOT placed on this page. If an editor later adds a product link, the placeholder `[Amazon-required Associate statement, wording pending verification]` goes directly under the intro and before the first link.
- Optional later "what makes it easier" notes (one per tier, by job, not by product) could point to the signature article's own shopping block rather than name gear here.
