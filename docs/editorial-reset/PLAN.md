## 00. STRATEGIC CHANGE (owner, 2026-11-19, after the audit approval): complete editorial rebuild. Supersedes the Stage 4 pilot scope and any 'rewrite' language in this plan.
We are not renovating the old articles; we are launching the publication as if it had never published. The founding editor-in-chief commissions a NEW library.
- **Archive, do not delete:** the original library is privately archived (`docs/editorial-reset/backup/original-articles/`, URL map included). Live pages stay until replacements are approved. Old bodies, outlines, headlines, product selections and formatting are NOT the foundation of new copy. Preserved for reuse: valuable URLs, verified research, sourced safety guidance, SEO research, infrastructure.
- **New editorial calendar:** based on audience desirability, brand alignment, organic search, Pinterest marketability, commercial potential, product research, emotional appeal, visual storytelling, customer usefulness, evergreen value, low maintenance. Original topics kept only where they are genuinely the strongest opportunities. 28 is a guideline, not a requirement. Inputs: brand identity, audience, six shopping collections, twelve signature experiences, interactive tools, commercial objectives.
- **Design the experience before writing** (per article: experience inspired; why she cares; what she learns/accomplishes; what makes her save or share; products she would genuinely want; photography that makes it desirable; links to collections and tools; why better than competing content).
- **Challenge the article presentation:** assess whether the current renderer and components support a premium editorial experience (editorial photography, curated shopping sections, project inspiration galleries, visual tutorials, comparison tables, complete experience guides); avoid technical complexity; keep CMS editing; articles must not all look or read the same. Aim: an independent lifestyle magazine with thoughtfully integrated commerce, not an affiliate blog with decorative photography.
- **Revised gate:** (1) technical simplification and ownership infrastructure continue unchanged (Stages 1 to 3); (2) in parallel: new editorial calendar, product research, creative direction; (3) PRESENT the proposed article inventory, editorial formats and initial creative concepts before committing to the full library; (4) then four exceptional pilot articles (chosen from the new inventory to span formats), integrated only after the product architecture and navigation are stable; (5) STOP for approval before any further articles.

## 7. Risks
- Rewriting everything resets source verification: every claim keeps its source or is dropped; safety content (cycling, clay, glue, skates) rewritten conservatively and re-sourced.

- Pilot may show the old drafts were already adequate in places: reuse where it adds value, per the brief.
- Volume: 28 articles x full pipeline is large; pilot sets the per-article cost before committing.
