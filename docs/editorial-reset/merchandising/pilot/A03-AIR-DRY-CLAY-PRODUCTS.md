A ring cone is a one-line "next" idea, not a fourth project.

Timeline anchor (SOURCED): 24 hours to the touch and 72 hours to dry completely, longer for thick pieces (S1, S5, S12); paint only on thoroughly dry pieces (S1); seal after 72 hours; test varnish compatibility first (S2). The article's timeline strip "Tonight / +24 h / +72 h" is supported.

Quantities (ESTIMATE, flag to the writer as such):
- Clay: one 1 kg (2.2 lb) block for all three, with margin for one restart. The only sourced anchor is that Mont Marte's tray tutorial uses a whole 500 g pack for one 22 x 12 cm tray (S6); our tray is smaller. A 500 g (1.1 lb) block is plausible if all three stay small, but leaves no margin: flag as the affordable-entry route, not the default.
- Paint: 3 to 4 colours plus white across all three; one small set is ample.
- Sealer: two to three thin coats per piece (S17, S14 both say thin coats); one small bottle.
- Household: rolling pin or straight-sided bottle, butter knife or craft knife, cookie cutter or card template, parchment or wax paper (S3), bowl of water, kitchen sponge, fine sandpaper, a soft brush (see 7).

**Look:** a matte flat lay on a cream or natural-wood ground (E3), one cherry note, at most two supporting tints (Strawberry, Butter or Matcha); gingham only as a prop. Product photography only when a licence is cleared (section 5); until then, typographic cards with no photograph and a styled-still-life hero labelled as inspiration, never as a product shot (E3).

**Grouping (three rows, in this order):**
1. **Start here: three things.** Clay, paint, sealer. One card each, numbered 1 to 3, with the job each does in the plan ("the clay", "the colour", "the finish").
2. **Already in your kitchen.** A quiet strip, not cards: rolling pin, butter knife, cutter, parchment, sponge, sandpaper.
3. **When you want more.** One card: the 20-colour set. No tool sets until one passes research.

**Tone:** specific, warm, dry; one detail and one honest limit per card; no "must-have"; no scores or stars; no "tested". Price bands appear as a small tag (Entry, Mid), never a figure.

**Mobile:** cards stack at 16 px gutters; the row order is the buying order.

**Sample product card (DAS, in Plans Past Five voice; every claim traceable to section 7 or 5.1):**
