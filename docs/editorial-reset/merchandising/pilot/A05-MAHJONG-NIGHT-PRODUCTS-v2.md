## 2. What the table needs (sourced)
American play: 152 tiles (36 each of dots, bams, cracks; 16 winds; 12 dragons; 8 flowers; 8 jokers), four players, one rack each, pushers optional, the current League card. Sets add spares (YMI Sapphire 166: 16 flowers, 10 jokers, 4 blanks, maker page). Rule for any listing: at least 8 jokers and 8 flowers. Tile sizes differ by maker (TML FAQ warns of rack misfit), so racks are bought with the set, never separately unless the maker confirms fit.
