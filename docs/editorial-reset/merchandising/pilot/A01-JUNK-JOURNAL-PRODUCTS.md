## 3. The decision, product and collection together

**Finding:** the functional core of a first spread is five jobs: a journal that takes glue, an adhesive, washi, a fine pen, small scissors. The "aesthetic" lives mostly in three surface choices: journal paper tone, washi colourway, and a decorative sheet. So the edit is **one core, three skins**, not three unrelated shopping lists. That keeps the basket small, keeps quantities right, and keeps A02 from duplicating it.

**Result of the 80-point gate:** five products pass on evidence (journal, runner, washi, pen, scissors). The decorative slot (stickers or ephemera) has NO product above 80 as researched. That is the honest state; section 9 says how to unblock it.

**Essentials (5):** journal, runner, washi set, pen, scissors. **Optional (1):** liquid glue for bulky or 3D bits (Tombow MONO Multi, 73, below the gate, kept out of the basket). **Not recommended in a first basket:** craft knife (sharp, unnecessary with scissors), highlighters, ink pads, a trimmer or mat (not researched in this pass), sticker books with 1,000+ pieces (against "small, pretty").

**Quantities for one first spread:** one journal (24 sheets is about a dozen two-page spreads); one runner (the maker lists 472 in of tape; far more than one spread needs); one 3-roll washi set (one roll is plenty); one pen set (or a single pen); one pair of scissors; zero or one decorative sheet; plus whatever scraps she already owns.

**Affordable entry versus upgrade (bands, no prices):**
- Entry: runner, washi set, one pen, scissors, onto a sturdy notebook she already owns (with the honest limit that thin paper will ripple). Bands: Entry to Considered.
- The edit: add the Strathmore journal (Considered). Total basket sits in Entry-to-Considered bands.
- Upgrades after a few evenings: a second journal (Stillman & Birn or Archer & Olive once verified), a second washi set, more pen nibs, a refill cartridge.

**Giftable box idea ("The First Spread Box"):** journal + runner + one washi set + one pen + (when verified) one Rifle sticker sheet, with a handwritten card listing the "what you already own" strip. Concept only; no box or packaging product has been researched.

**Replenishables:** runner refill cartridge (Tombow says "snap-in refill cartridge"; a 3-pack refill SKU 62207 was seen in a search result, unverified), washi, pens, stickers. The journal repeats when full.

**Free ephemera strip (secondary, per E3 voice, one line each, after the paid picks):** old envelopes, ticket stubs, receipts, tea-bag packets, pages from a book you do not mind cutting. Sources: Pipsticks and Tombow guidance (above); the specific list is editorial.

## 4. Three directions, named

Names are working titles in the E3 palette vocabulary. **Trademark and existing-use checks not done.**

1. **Postmarked.** Warm tan pages, black ink, black-and-metallic washi. Mood: a letter you meant to send; travel scraps. Pairs with a Tim Holtz ephemera pack if it clears verification.
2. **Garden Press.** Warm tan pages, a cherry-mint-butter striped washi, a floral sticker sheet. Mood: pressed flowers and a florist's wrapping. The only direction with a decorative sheet today.
3. **Sunday Blue.** Cool gray pages, blue washi, nothing else. Mood: calm, nearly architectural. The restraint is the point, and it fits "no unnecessary items".
