## 1. What changed from v1, in six points

1. **Amazon is now a gate, not a score.** A product is eligible only if an `amazon.com/dp` (or clp) URL carrying its ASIN was seen in search. Every such ASIN is a **lead, unverified**; the verified-ASIN column is empty for every product; status is **needs Cowork Amazon verification**. 2. **Kokuyo SAXA scissors are out.** Amazon.com listings do exist, but they are titled "Japan Import", show no Kokuyo or authorised US seller, and the models differ from the maker pages we read; Kokuyo's own page points to Amazon Japan. 3. **The decorative-sheet slot now has a genuine Amazon candidate:** Rifle Paper Co. Planner Sticker Set (v1 wrongly said it was not on Amazon). It is conditional on contents and seller verification.

The mt sets now have dp URLs, and one listing is reported as sold by a third-party reseller, which is a new gate risk for W1.

## 7. Complete beginner kits versus buying separately

Four Amazon kits were compared (K1 to K4 below). **None passes 80.**

- **K1 The Ultimate Junk Journaling Kit (Chartwell Books, ISBN-10 0785851542): 71, Research further.** The most credible kit found: it is a recognisable publisher's boxed kit, and the listing (Amazon search text, unverified) describes a 32-page project book, a 96-page blank journal, 25 scrapbook sheets, 3 washi rolls and 10 sticker sheets. Against buying separately: it is plausibly the cheaper route and gives a ready gift, but we cannot tell the journal's paper weight, the washi's quality or whether the stickers are decorative reproductions; it has no adhesive named; the sheet counts are paper-heavy for a "small, pretty" edit. Our separate basket gives three makers with stated specs. K1 is the one worth a Cowork look: if the physical kit's paper weight, adhesive and washi can be confirmed, it could become the "ready-made gift kit" slot.
- **K2 Scrap't (fabric A5 journal, 200+ pieces, 64): Reject** (unknown brand; includes tape roller and glue dots; paper weight unknown).
- **K3 Draupnir 346-piece kit: Reject** (private label, thin grid notebook, 346 pieces).
- **K4 Printed ephemera "kit" books (no journal): Reject** (paper only; needs home printing).

Honest conclusion: **the kit slot is empty.** The "First Spread Box" (section 10) is the gift answer: it uses only specced products.
