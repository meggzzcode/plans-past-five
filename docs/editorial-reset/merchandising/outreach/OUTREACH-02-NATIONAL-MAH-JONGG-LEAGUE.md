4. Cards at the table: whether the League recommends one card per player.
5. Imagery and use: whether we may photograph a closed card (cover only, no hands shown) for editorial use, and any conditions or credit line you require. We will never reproduce the card's hands.
6. Whether the League has an affiliate or referral arrangement; we are not seeking an agreement.
