## How to read this

- **Event elevator** = hosting, ambience, serving, decor or game gear that lifts a social occasion. **Hobby helper** = supplies that make starting a hobby easier and more enjoyable.
- **start** = the beginner buy; **step-up** = an intermediate upgrade for people who get hooked (never expert gear).
- **Existing** means the product is already in docs/commerce/product-research.csv or docs/commerce/kit-candidates-for-cowork.csv (existing product id given). **New** means a proposal with no id yet.
- **Evidence strength** is stated on every pick. Most sources I could reach were affiliate-supported roundups and retailer or maker pages; Wirecutter, NYT and several Cornell/Section Hiker pages returned 403 or could not be fetched, so some are search-snippet only and are marked so. Cowork should re-open each source URL, and must confirm Amazon-listing sentiment (qualitatively only): I could not read Amazon here.
- Existing catalog products mostly rest on listing-level evidence. Where a better-evidenced equivalent exists I recommend it first and keep the existing one as a fallback.
