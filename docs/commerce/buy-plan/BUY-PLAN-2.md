# Buy plan, set 2: published articles 12 to 22 (alphabetical)

Companion file: `candidates-2.csv` (50 rows, one per article/slot/tier). Research and planning only: no catalog, article or source files were changed.

## Method and honesty notes
- Scope: the 11 published articles numbered 12 to 22 by file name in `content/articles`: find-a-hobby, recreational cycling, craft night, mahjong night, paint night, picnic, solo Saturday, book club, reading journal, air-dry clay, crocheting.
- Rules applied: ordinary Amazon.com products only; ASIN and URL left blank for Cowork; no prices, ratings or review counts anywhere; no alcohol, no health claims; price judged by tier (budget / mid) only; no affiliate activation.
- Evidence: I could not read Amazon. Several editorial sites (REI, Wirecutter) blocked or did not return, so many source notes come from search-result summaries of buying guides. Every row states its evidence strength. All "Cowork to confirm sentiment" items need an Amazon listing-level check (qualitative only: widely praised for X, common complaint Y).
- Safety notes are general and sourced to the articles' existing authorities (CPSC, BHSI, ACMI, NHTSA, Crayola). Cowork must still read each manufacturer page before copy is written.
- Existing catalog products were reused wherever they fit (21 unique existing IDs). Many existing items have status `candidate` (not yet rendering publicly) and have no independent sentiment research; they are flagged.
- Shared new picks (Field Notes, Sakura Koi, DAS, Soundcore speaker, Hario teapot, Baggu) appear on more than one article; add once to the catalog, attach to several articles.
