# Image and asset rights notes (C10), 2026-11-18
Default: photo-less product cards. No product photo is proposed for publication in this pass, so nothing below is approved for use.

## Amazon product images
- Source read: Associates Program Policies / Operating Agreement text (https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies, updated April 14, 2026, read 2026-11-18). "Program Content" (which includes images obtained from Amazon) is licensed only for use in connection with the Associates Program and may not be altered except proportional resizing; Program Content may not be used to promote products offered on non-Amazon sites; the license is tied to the Associate's own account.
- Consequence: Amazon images are not ours to publish or to transfer to a buyer. They are available only through Amazon's own tools (Creators API / approved link tools) under the Associate's own agreement. Verdict: DO NOT use Amazon product images; this also matches the "no SiteStripe, no scraping" ground rule.
- Not verified: whether the Creators API image license permits storing or caching images (not read). Do not assume.

## Manufacturer and brand photos
- No manufacturer image terms were read (manufacturer pages were not opened for most candidates; see product-research-notes.md). Brand images are presumed all-rights-reserved. Verdict: do not use unless a brand's press kit or affiliate page states permission AND the permission survives a sale (an affiliate/press license is usually personal and non-transferable). Record the exact terms here before any use.

## Retailer and affiliate-network creatives
- Banner and product-feed images from Awin, impact, CJ, Rakuten and similar are normally licensed to the approved publisher under that program's terms and are not transferable to a site buyer. Not read in detail. Verdict: not used.

## Stock photography (not researched)
- Candidates such as Unsplash, Pexels and Pixabay were NOT read in this pass. Before any use: read the current license page, record the URL and date, and confirm (a) commercial use, (b) no attribution needed or attribution is supplied, (c) restrictions on identifiable people or brand logos in the frame, (d) whether the license passes to a buyer of the site. Photos of identifiable people carry extra model-release risk.

## Existing site images

I did not verify the tool's output terms or whether ownership/transfer rights are stated. OPEN QUESTION for the owner: record the Lovable terms page URL and date confirming commercial use and transferability, and keep the generation dates.

## Recommendation
Ship photo-less cards (name, "what it's for", search/ASIN link only). Revisit images only with licensed or self-made photos and a signed or published license on file.
