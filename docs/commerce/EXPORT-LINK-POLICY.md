# Export link policy (Amazon Associates), read 2026-11-19

Sources fetched 2026-11-19:
- Operating Agreement: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement (reached)
- Program Policies: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies (reached)

## What the pages say (quoted from the fetch summaries)
- Policies, Participation Requirements s.4: "You will not engage in any Promotional Activities in any offline manner" and no use of "any Special Link in connection with any printed material, ebook, mailing, or any oral solicitation."
- Policies s.4: "You may include Special Links in emails, SMS and direct messaging from your social media Sites" (solicited messages only).
- Policies s.2(a)-(b): "you are permitted to display Special Links on your Site"; "All Special Links must be accessed directly from your Site."
- Policies, Trademark Guidelines s.3: Amazon marks not in "offline material or email (e.g., in any printed material, mailing, SMS, MMS, attachment to email".
- Agreement: Special Links must "properly use the special 'tagged' link formats"; Program Content may be used "on your Site or any other location where Amazon may authorize". The Agreement itself has no offline/PDF/e-book sentence.
- PDFs and downloads are not addressed separately; they are treated as documents/printed material here (conservative reading).

The fetch tool summarises pages, so wording is from a summary; the buyer must re-read the live pages before going live.

## Rule adopted
Copied text, printed lists, downloads, exports and shares never contain a tagged or any Amazon link. They carry product names plus a link back to our own page (src/lib/export-links.ts). On-site buttons only use the monetizable resolver.
