## How the 50 were picked

The expansion theme list had exactly 50 entries. To avoid near-duplicates, 7 were dropped or merged and 7 replacement topics were split out of higher-value themes, so the final count is still 50.

Dropped or merged, with reasons:
- **Needlepoint**: near-duplicate intent with embroidery (`how-to-start-embroidery`); search intent overlap is likely (guess). Mention inside the embroidery article instead.
- **Book journaling**: duplicates `how-to-start-a-reading-journal`.
- **Seasonal crafts** (generic): too broad to be one intent; pressed flowers was split out as a distinct topic.
- **Affordable hobby supplies**: overlaps `affordable-hobbies-under-25` and the thrifting topic.
- **Picnic parties**: a spoke of `how-to-plan-a-perfect-picnic`, not a separate intent.
- **Seasonal hosting** (generic): too vague; replaced by a specific tea-party topic and the holiday topic.
- **At-home retreats**: overlaps `at-home-creative-weekend` and `how-to-plan-a-solo-saturday`.

Split-out replacements: paint-your-own pottery night, cozy reading nook, pressed flower crafts, sketchbook habit, tea party at home, walking dates with friends, book swap. Each is a guess at a distinct reader intent and must be checked.

Weak or cautious topics kept at low priority: yoga (health-claims risk; no medical claims allowed), roller skating and candle making (safety claims need official sources), dance classes and antique markets (low commercial fit).

## Rules for promoting a topic

- No invented experience, testing, prices, ratings or ASINs.
- Do not promote a topic whose only value is a product link.
- Do not publish two articles with the same intent. If a candidate's intent matches a published article, fold it in.
