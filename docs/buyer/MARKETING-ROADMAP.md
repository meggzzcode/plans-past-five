# Marketing roadmap

Pre-revenue and not yet public (2026-11-19). This is a sequence of work, not a forecast. No traffic, follower, ranking or income outcomes are promised. Detailed channel notes: `docs/marketing/PINTEREST-STRATEGY.md`, `docs/marketing/CONTENT-DISTRIBUTION.md`, `docs/buyer/SEO-GUIDE.md`.

## Phase 0: before promoting anything

- Site live on your domain with HTTPS (`docs/buyer/HOSTING-AND-DEPLOYMENT.md`).
- Contact email, Privacy, Terms reviewed.
- Search Console verified, sitemap submitted (`docs/buyer/ANALYTICS-SETUP.md`).
- Publish enough verified articles that a visitor finds depth: start with the flagships and their spokes (`docs/content/ARTICLE-ROADMAP.md`).

## Phase 1: foundation (organic search plus Pinterest)

1. Publish verified articles in clusters; each with inbound internal links.
2. Create the three pin concepts per article; use the prepared pin assets and CSV (`public/pins`, `docs/pinterest-pins.csv`).
3. Open and verify a Pinterest business account in your own name; create boards matching the six sections.
4. Check Search Console weekly for indexing issues.

## Phase 2: learn what works

- Compare which articles and pins draw readers who stay and save checklists (analytics once enabled).
- Update or merge weak articles instead of adding more of the same.
- Promote winning topics to deeper guides from the candidate list (`docs/buyer/CONTENT-ROADMAP.md`).

## Phase 3: commerce (only with your own approvals)

- Apply for affiliate programs once the site is live (`docs/buyer/AFFILIATE-SETUP.md`).
- Verify products and fill kits slowly (`docs/commerce/PRODUCT-RESEARCH.md`).
- Re-run the link and disclosure check (`docs/SOP-affiliate-link-check.md`).
