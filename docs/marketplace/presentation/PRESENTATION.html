02 · Target audience

Women who want a hobby, a plan, a friend.

The intended reader is a working woman who is ready for more outside of work and wants help getting started: what to try, what it costs, what to do on Saturday, who to do it with.

After the office

Weeknight hobbies that do not need a full Saturday or a second wind.

Solo, not lonely

Dates with yourself, from bookstore wanders to a café window seat.

Meeting people

Hobbies that come with friends: craft nights, book clubs, a standing tennis date.

Beginners, on a budget

"Being good at it is completely optional." Starter costs shown as estimates.

This is an editorial positioning choice, not research. There is no audience data, survey or analytics behind it.

Mobile first: all pages were captured at 390 px as well as 1440 px.

09 · Affiliate infrastructure

Built to be switched on with your accounts.

Not activated

Every entry ships empty: no tracking IDs, active: false, no approvals. Links are plain retailer searches and the disclosure says links currently earn nothing.

  • One central registry: src/config/affiliate.ts is the only place retailer links are built
  • Resolution order: approved and active entry, else ordinary retailer URL, else plain search link
  • Amazon Associates (the only program planned) are tracked in a research register (unverified until read from official pages)
  • Seller's master and portfolio accounts are excluded. No IDs, links or sharing with any other site
  • Approval is not guaranteed and is tied to the buyer's own account and site

Left: the public disclosure page. Right: a collection page, where links would be tagged only after activation.

Activation guide: docs/buyer/AFFILIATE-ACTIVATION.md (step by step) and AFFILIATE-SETUP.md (checklist). Edit the registry, run the tests, and the disclosure switches automatically to the monetized form.
