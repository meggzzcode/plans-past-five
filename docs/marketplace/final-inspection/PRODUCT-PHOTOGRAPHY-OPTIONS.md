# Product photography options (Cowork research, 2026-11-19)
Scope: ways to show attractive product pictures on Plans Past Five with no paid API, no recurring fee and no complicated dependency. Research only: no image downloaded or stored, no request sent. NOT FOUND means I searched and the page or term did not turn up in the pages read.

## Summary recommendation
1. **Amazon SiteStripe images are not available** (SiteStripe offers only Short Link / Full Link today; see SITESTRIPE-IMAGE-FORMAT.md). Amazon product images are Program Content: no alteration, use only on the listed Site, and the only sanctioned image feed is API based (not pursued). Do not copy Amazon photos.
2. Best low-cost route: **(d) the owner's own photography** of a few purchased items plus original lifestyle imagery, labelled honestly. It costs a few purchases, needs nobody's permission, and a buyer can keep using it.
3. Add **(a) manufacturer permission** only for a short list where a brand has a press or media contact (Tombow, Colart for Liquitex and Winsor & Newton are the clearest). Written permission is per brand, may be revocable, and may not transfer to a buyer: record it and tell the buyer.
4. Keep the shop text-first (swatch cards) for any product without a permitted photo.

## Option (a) manufacturer or press photography
Cost: free to ask. Permission: the brand's marketing/PR contact. Maintenance: permission letter must be kept with the site and may need re-confirming for a new owner. Risk: reuse limits; a brand can decline or attach conditions (credit line, no alteration, use only for editorial reviews).

## Option (b) licensed product photography
Stock libraries almost never show the exact branded product. Free licences (CC0) will not carry brand-accurate packshots; one-off paid licences are for generic items (blank notebook, tape) and would imply no brand. Cost: free to a few dollars per image. Risk: low for generic items, high if a licence image shows a different brand than the recommended product. Recommendation: use only for lifestyle scenes, never as product proof.

## Option (c) affiliate imagery (Amazon)
- SiteStripe: Short Link and Full Link only; no Text+Image or embed (read 2026-11-19).
- Policy: Program Content may not be altered (resize in proportion only), only on the Site, non-transferable licence. Hotlinking/storage rules beyond this: NOT FOUND in the pages read.
- API-served images exist but depend on the Creators/PA API and its sales eligibility, which the owner ruled out for this pass.
- Verdict: not available as a no-cost, compliant option today. Re-check SiteStripe options after the account has qualifying sales.

## Option (d) original imagery
Cost: the products (some are already bought) and a phone. Permission: none for own photos; keep dated originals. Maintenance: lowest; a buyer owns the files. Risk: must not imply hands-on testing if items were only photographed; label lifestyle shots as illustrative and never imply a specific branded product in an inspirational image (AI-generated or staged). Recommendation: primary route for the 8 to 12 hero products.
