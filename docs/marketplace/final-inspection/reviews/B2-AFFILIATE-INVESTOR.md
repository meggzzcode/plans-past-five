## What would PREVENT an offer
1. No live domain, no indexing, no traffic or Search Console data: it is a code-and-docs bundle, not a site with any signal.
2. Amazon Associates is non-transferable and not applied for; I would be the one to risk the 180-day qualification on a brand-new AI-heavy site.
3. Domain ownership unconfirmed (placeholder) and trademark not cleared.
4. AI images with unverified transfer terms and no copyright; AI-drafted content with no human-authority or first-hand angle.
5. No keyword or competitor evidence; I cannot tell whether any article targets winnable queries.
6. Asking price anchor of $2,950 not supported by any verified sale (seller admits this).
7. Unrun secrets scan and doc/count inconsistencies.

## What would make me offer
- A live domain the seller owns and pushes, with 8-12 weeks of Search Console showing impressions and indexed pages on the 27 articles.
- Keyword/SERP data per article showing low-competition targets.
- Evidence the images and drafts can be used commercially (tool terms), or human-replaced key images.
- A second monetization path pre-wired and verified (another network or display-ad ready), and a clear price near replacement-cost-of-labour only.
- Clean history scan, reconciled counts, LICENSE file.

## What I would pay (order of magnitude)
Low hundreds to roughly $1,000-$1,500 USD, as of today's state. Reasoning: no earnings, traffic or links, so no multiple applies. Value is the saved labour of 27 drafted articles, 4 tools, brand and pins, discounted heavily because AI content is cheap to reproduce and the Amazon and SEO risk all transfers to me. The seller's $1,500-$2,500 expectation would require a live, indexing site; the $2,950 ask is above what I would pay. This is an opinion, not a market fact.
