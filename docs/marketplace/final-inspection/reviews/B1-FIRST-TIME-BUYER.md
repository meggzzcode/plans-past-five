## What would PREVENT me making an offer
1. I cannot run it alone: building, deploying, editing hobbies/products and fixing errors need a developer or AI coding tool. Browser CMS is "configured but not proven" (nobody has logged in and edited a real article).
2. All 18 site images and all pins with photos are AI-generated; no exclusivity or registrable copyright; image-tool transfer terms unverified (Canva pages returned 403, not read). Replacing images is my cost.
3. Not live, no domain, no host, no email: I would do all setup, and the contact page shows no way for readers to reach me.
4. Seller secrets scan on repo history is listed as not yet run.
5. Documents disagree on counts (hobbies 36 vs 43; tests 250/523/629/641) and the listing is still marked DRAFT with open owner decisions; this lowers my trust in the paperwork.
6. Legal pages are starter text needing lawyer review; Amazon approval for a brand-new site with no traffic is uncertain.
7. Operating costs not stated by seller as a firm number.

## What would make me offer
- A very low, clearly "starter kit" price; seller-registered domain pushed to me; a live deployment I could see working under my own accounts, with a recorded walkthrough of a real edit and deploy.
- Confirmed CMS test; clean secrets scan; one consistent set of counts; written image rights position; 30-day support extended or a paid onboarding package.

## What I would pay (order of magnitude, hypothetical)
Hundreds of dollars (low hundreds up to roughly $1,000), reasoning only from replacement effort and my risk: I would be buying files and a plan, not traffic or income, I would need to pay or learn to operate it, and rights on images are uncertain. Not thousands. No comparable sales or revenue assumed.

## Questions for the seller
1. Do you own planspastfive.com, and will you push it to me? Is the name cleared anywhere?
2. Can you show a fresh deploy to a host, with me watching, and a real article edit through the CMS?
3. Which tool generated each image, and what do its terms say about transfer and commercial use? Will you indemnify any image claim?
4. What exact support is included beyond 5 hours of written Q&A? Can you do the first deploy for me?
5. Has the repo history been scanned for secrets? Which seller accounts, keys or IDs remain in files?
6. What is the real monthly cost you expect for the cheapest working setup, and what happens when the free host hits its cap?
7. Which 4 articles are unchecked, and which claims need review before launch?
8. Why are you really selling, and have you launched other sites from this template?
