# Usability test script for real human reviewers (3 to 5 women, 20 minutes each)

Use real people only with their consent; record no names, emails or faces. Do not coach. Ask them to think aloud. Phone first (their own phone), then 5 minutes on a laptop.

Say: "You are not being tested; the website is. There are no wrong answers. Say what you are thinking."

## Tasks (read one at a time)
1. Open the site. In one sentence, what is this and who is it for? What would you tap first?
2. You have 20 minutes and low energy tonight. Find one thing to do.
3. Take the hobby quiz. Did the result feel like you? Would you save anything?
4. Pick a creative hobby you would try. Read the article. Do you know how to start? What would you buy first?
5. Find where to buy it. Would you tap "View on Amazon"? Why or why not? (Do not buy.)
6. Plan a Saturday with a friend for about $40. Is the plan something you would actually do? Share it the way you normally would.
7. Close the site and reopen it. Find what you saved.
8. How does this site make money? How would you contact the people who run it?

## Record per task (no names)
Done unaided / done with hesitation / failed; seconds taken; exact words of confusion; anything that felt fake, repetitive or "AI"; anything that felt delightful.

## End questions (1-10, one sentence why)
First impression; looks; easy to use; feels written by real people; useful; trust; would you buy from the picks; would you come back; would you send it to a friend.

## Rules for reading results
Treat 5 people as signals, not statistics. Fix anything two or more people stumble on. Report simulated (AI) reviews separately from human ones.
