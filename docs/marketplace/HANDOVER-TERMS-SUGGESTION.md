# Suggested post-sale handover terms

**This is a suggestion, not legal advice.** The owner and a lawyer must review and adapt it. Marketplace rules may also apply: Flippa's handover and domain-transfer rules were NOT READ (FLIPPA-RESEARCH.md), and its terms say sellers must not circumvent the platform (https://flippa.com/terms-of-service, read 2026-11-18). No indefinite free support.

## Suggested terms

1. **Fixed term.** A short, fixed handover window starting at acceptance and ending on a stated date. The length is for the parties to decide; a few weeks is a common-sense starting point. Put the end date in writing. No automatic extension.
2. **Scope.** Factual questions about the repo, the docs in `docs/buyer`, how content and data files work, and the transfer steps in `docs/buyer/TRANSFER-CHECKLIST.md`.
3. **Channel.** Written messages only, one agreed channel, with a reasonable response time stated by the seller.
4. **Limits.** The seller does not operate the site, write content, run accounts, set up the buyer's Amazon Associates or payment accounts, or fix bugs beyond answering questions, unless separately agreed and paid.
5. **No guarantees.** No guarantee of traffic, rankings, Amazon Associates approval, income or sales. The site is pre-revenue.
6. **No access to buyer accounts.** The seller never asks for buyer passwords. The buyer never receives seller credentials; transfers use the marketplace and registrar processes.
7. **Records.** Both parties keep copies of messages.
8. **Completion.** After the end date the seller has no further obligation. Seller access is removed everywhere and shared credentials rotated (TRANSFER-CHECKLIST steps 15 and 20).
9. **Extra help.** Any help outside the scope or after the end date is a separate written agreement, priced by the parties.

## Review items for the lawyer

Governing law, warranties and disclaimers, what is warranted about ownership of assets (domain, images), treatment of AI-generated images, confidentiality, any non-compete, and consistency with the marketplace's terms.
