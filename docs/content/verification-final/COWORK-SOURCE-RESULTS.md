**how-to-plan-a-perfect-picnic** (8 sources)
- A great picnic is a blanket in the shade, food that stayed cold, a jar of flowers and nobody wishing they'd brought an opener.
- Add one thing with height or softness: a small bunch of flowers in a jar (the [flower arranging guide](/journal/flower-arranging-as-a-hobby) shows how), a cushion, a tall bottle.
- As a general guide, keep cold food at or below 40 °F (4 °C) with ice packs or plenty of ice in an insulated cooler, and keep the cooler in the shade and closed as much as you can; a separate cooler for drinks means the food cooler gets opened less.
- Don't leave perishables, such as meat, dairy, cut fruit and cooked dishes, out for more than two hours, or one hour when it's above 90 °F (32 °C).
- Keep raw meat separate from everything else, and don't reuse a platter or utensils that held raw meat for cooked food.
- Hand sanitizer with at least 60% alcohol is a backup for when soap and water aren't available, and it doesn't remove food allergens.
