# Canva licence terms for AI-generated images (read 2026-11-19)
Terms only, no legal conclusion. Pages read through search snippets and one page fetch on 2026-11-19; re-read the live page before relying on any line.

## What Canva says
- **Ownership.** AI Product Terms (https://www.canva.com/policies/ai-product-terms/, snippet): "As between you and Canva, and to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, you retain your ownership rights to your Input, and you own your Output, except for any Output that modifies or incorporates Licensed Content". The page's own age note read "217 days ago"; no effective date is printed on the page.
- **Commercial use.** Canva Help, "Use Canva to design digital and physical products for sale" (https://www.canva.com/help/using-canva-to-create-products-for-sale/): AI-generated images "are not covered by Canva's Content License Agreement. You can use these images for any lawful purpose, including selling them, as long as you comply with our AI Product Terms." Help, "How to use Magic Studio safely and legally" (https://www.canva.com/help/using-magic-studio-safely-and-legally/): "You can use AI-created works for personal and commercial purposes, as long as you comply with our AI Product Terms."
- **Exclusivity.** Same help page: "Anyone can get similar results to you, if they use similar prompts"; Canva "can't guarantee your AI-created work will be unique". Help "Do I own images I generated using AI?" (https://www.canva.com/help/copyright-design-ownership/): ownership is "between you and Canva, to the extent permitted by law"; whether the image has copyright protection "will depend on the l[aw]" (snippet cut off). The selling page adds: "In many countries, the legal ownership of AI-generated works is unclear. This means you may not be the copyright owner of the images you make."
- **Canva AI page** (https://www.canva.com/canva-ai/): "Canva AI can be used for both personal and commercial projects"; "if you use AI output for commercial projects, you may not have exclusive rights to your AI-generated design".
- **Recognizable people, characters or brands.** Magic Media help (https://www.canva.com/help/using-magic-media/): permission is needed if the design includes them.

## The Content License Agreement does not cover AI output
https://www.canva.com/policies/content-license-agreement/ (fetched 2026-11-19): the page does not mention Magic Media or AI output and prints no effective date. Its section 9 says you can't "sub-license, re-sell, rent, lend, assign, gift or otherwise transfer or distribute the Content", with an exception in section 4A to transfer a design to a single client for their own use. That agreement governs Canva stock Content, not AI output; do not apply it to our images, and none of our images should include Canva stock elements.

## Transfer with a sold site: NOT STATED
No page read says anything about assigning or transferring AI output to a buyer of a website. "You own your Output" is the closest wording and it is limited by "to the extent permitted by law". Record the terms in the sale packet and let the buyer judge; do not state that the licence is transferable.
