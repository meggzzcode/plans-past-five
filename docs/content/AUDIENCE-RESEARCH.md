# Audience research (C1), 2026-11-18

What this file is: a short, honest record of what the research pass could and could not establish. Per-article findings are in the "Cowork research 2026-11-18" section of each `docs/content/briefs/<slug>.md`.

## What was done
- Live web searches for the primary keyword of the 9 highest-priority articles (junk journaling x2, air-dry clay, watercolor, starter kits, embroidery, crochet, reading journal, mahjong night). Top-result URLs were recorded in each brief. Page content was read from search result text only, not opened in full.
- The remaining 21 briefs carry the structure with every field marked NOT FOUND or NOT DONE; competitor tables are empty.

## What could not be done (and why)
- No search-volume, difficulty or trend numbers: no keyword tool or Google Trends access in this session. None are stated anywhere.
- No autocomplete, People Also Ask or related-search captures: not available from the search tool.
- No Pinterest suggestions: needs the owner's Pinterest login; stopped per the ground rules.
- No community questions (Reddit, forums): not collected in this pass.
- The 50-topic buyer roadmap (docs/buyer/CONTENT-ROADMAP.md): Demand and Competition cells are still PENDING COWORK and no topics were dropped, because no evidence exists to justify dropping any.

## Qualitative signals seen across the top results (not demand data)
- Beginner guides across junk journaling, clay, embroidery and crochet all lead with "use what you already have" and a short supply list; several quote a starter budget (e.g. under $20 or $30-50) without sourcing it. A honest, sourced, tiered budget is a visible gap.
- Many top pages are retailer or brand blogs with product funnels; affiliate roundups dominate "best kits" results (see creative-hobby-starter-kits brief).
- Mahjong-night results emphasise stating the variant and card year in the invite and pairing new with experienced players; variant statements must be re-checked against nationalmahjonggleague.org each year.
