# Research brief: flower-arranging-as-a-hobby

Status: draft. Answers below come from reasoning only. No data has been collected; every research section is pending.

1. **Why would someone search this?** Someone buys grocery-store flowers and wants them to look intentional, and searches for simple arrangement recipes.
2. **Why would someone save it on Pinterest?** Before-and-after bouquet images and step recipes are highly saveable, particularly around spring and holidays.
3. **What problem or aspiration does it serve?** Problem: bunches that look flat or die fast. Aspiration: a pretty table and a weekly ritual.
4. **What products would genuinely help?** A clean vase, floral snips, flower food, tape or wire grid and a tray. No catalog products exist yet.
5. **What would she reasonably need to buy?** A bunch of flowers and a jar. Typically about $10 to $25 a week, to be verified.
6. **What might she already own?** A jug, jars, kitchen scissors.
7. **What optional products help?** Snips, narrow vase, chicken wire, tray.
8. **What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?** A focal, filler and greenery recipe, flower care basics and a pet-toxicity warning pointing to a trusted list.
9. **Which other articles should we link?** creative-hobbies-you-can-start-at-home, things-to-do-after-work-instead-of-scrolling, weekend-edit-planner printable.
10. **How does it contribute to long-term revenue?** Seasonal peaks around spring and holidays. A verified vase and snips pass would add modest affiliate fit.
