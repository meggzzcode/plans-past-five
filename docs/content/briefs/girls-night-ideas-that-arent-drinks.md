# Research brief: 25 Girls' Night Ideas That Aren't Just Going Out for Drinks

Slug: girls-night-ideas-that-arent-drinks
Status: written from editorial reasoning only. No search, traffic or price data has been collected; every data section below is pending Cowork.

## Why would someone search this?
She is organizing a friend night and wants options beyond bars, often because friends don't drink, are saving money or want quieter plans.

## Why would someone save it on Pinterest?
Numbered idea lists for group plans are highly shareable and pinned for later planning, with seasonal peaks around holidays and birthdays.

## Problem and aspiration
Problem: the group chat defaults to drinks. Aspiration: friendships built around doing things together.

## Products that would genuinely help
Group painting kits, cookie decorating kits, board games, conversation cards, friendship-bracelet kits.

## What would she reasonably need to buy?
Depends on the plan; many ideas need only a table and snacks.

## What might she already own?
Table, snacks, music, board games, mugs, a blanket.

## Optional products that help
A craft kit, conversation cards, a board game.

## What makes our recommendations better than generic shopping results
Cost bands, group sizes, how to get a chat to commit, inclusion advice and no named venues or brands. Products are described by what they help her do, claims are hedged and checked, and nothing is recommended unless it is verified.
