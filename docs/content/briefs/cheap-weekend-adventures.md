# Research brief: 25 Affordable Weekend Adventures

Slug: `cheap-weekend-adventures`. Status: draft. Written from reasoning only; no external data has been gathered.

## Why would someone search this?
She wants to do something interesting without spending much. 'Cheap weekend activities' and 'free things to do this weekend' signal a budget-conscious planner who is happy to be creative.

## Why would someone save it on Pinterest?
A numbered list of adventures by category, with outdoorsy and walking imagery, saves well to boards like 'Weekend plans' and 'Things to do on a budget'.

## What problem or aspiration does it serve?
Problem: feeling that fun costs money. Aspiration: a town that feels like a destination.

## What products would genuinely help?
A small daypack, a pocket notebook, a market tote, binoculars (later), comfortable walking shoes.

## What would she reasonably need to buy?
Nothing to begin. Water, a snack and comfortable shoes.

## What might she already own?
Tote, notebook, bottle, spare layer, sketch pencil.

## What optional products help?
Daypack, pocket notebook, binoculars.

## What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?
Categories of places rather than named venues, so it works in any town, with a find-your-own prompt for each and honest 'check local rules' notes. Generic lists name a handful of famous places that are irrelevant to most readers.

## Which other articles should link to and from it?

## How does it contribute to long-term revenue?
Evergreen top-of-funnel traffic and a feeder into the weekend planner, hobby pages (hiking, birdwatching, thrifting) and a small set of gear categories. Revenue unquantified until data exists.
