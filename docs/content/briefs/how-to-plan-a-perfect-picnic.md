# Research brief: How to Plan the Perfect Aesthetic Picnic

Slug: `how-to-plan-a-perfect-picnic`. Status: draft. Written from reasoning only; no external data has been gathered.

## Why would someone search this?
She has a good day, a friend or date in mind, and the image of a beautiful picnic she does not know how to build. 'Aesthetic picnic' and 'how to plan a picnic' mix inspiration with logistics.

## Why would someone save it on Pinterest?
Picnics are a perennial, highly visual pin category. Styling plus a packing checklist is saved in spring and summer, and pinned again as boards for dates, birthdays and friend catch-ups.

## What problem or aspiration does it serve?
Problem: picnics that fall flat (soggy food, small blanket, wrong gear). Aspiration: a lovely, relaxed afternoon that also looks good.

## What products would genuinely help?
A large waterproof-backed blanket, a picnic basket or sturdy tote, an insulated bag, reusable tableware and cloth napkins, lidded containers or jars.

## What would she reasonably need to buy?
Realistically a good blanket and some way to keep food cool. The rest is optional.

## What might she already own?
Large throw or blanket, tote, plates, cutlery, jars, napkins, a bottle opener.

## What optional products help?
Basket, cushions, reusable tableware, sketchbook, a small speaker.

## What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?
Food-safety guidance that is hedged and points to official sources, a timeline, spot-scouting tips and a clear own/buy/later split. Generic results show a basket with a price; we explain which pieces change the experience.
