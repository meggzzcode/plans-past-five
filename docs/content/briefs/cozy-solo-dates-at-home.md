## Why would someone search this?
She is tired, broke or a homebody and wants an evening in that feels intentional rather than default scrolling.

## Why would someone save it on Pinterest?
Cozy-aesthetic ideas for rainy days and low-energy evenings have strong seasonal save appeal, especially in autumn and winter.

## Problem and aspiration
Problem: low energy and evening drift. Aspiration: a night in that feels like a treat.

## Products that would genuinely help
Book lights, junk journals, air-dry clay, reading journals, washi tape.

## What would she reasonably need to buy?
Very little; most ideas use things already at home.

## What might she already own?
Pajamas, a lamp, candles, a puzzle, a book, kitchen basics, paper and stamps.

## Optional products that help
A book light, a junk journal and washi tape, a small air-dry clay set.

## What makes our recommendations better than generic shopping results
Energy-based sorting, a phone rule, safety notes (candles, clay, plants and pets, skincare labels) and hedged costs. Products are described by what they help her do, claims are hedged and checked, and nothing is recommended unless it is verified.
