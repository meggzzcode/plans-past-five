# Research brief: watercolor-painting-for-beginners

Status: draft. Answers below come from reasoning only. No data has been collected; every research section is pending.

1. **Why would someone search this?** A beginner wants to know the minimum supplies and the first techniques, often after feeling put off by how fussy watercolor looks.
2. **Why would someone save it on Pinterest?** Swatch cards, gradient washes and simple floral painting tutorials are widely saved. Technique and project lists perform as pins.
3. **What problem or aspiration does it serve?** Problem: not knowing what paper and paint to buy or how to start. Aspiration: pretty paintings without drawing ability.
4. **What products would genuinely help?** Watercolor paper or sketchbooks, a small pan set, one round brush, a portable travel set and masking tape.
5. **What would she reasonably need to buy?** A small set, one brush and decent paper. Typically about $20 to $50, to be verified.
6. **What might she already own?** Plate for mixing, jar for water, cloth, a hard surface to work on.
7. **What optional products help?** Second brush, travel set, fine pen, masking tape.
8. **What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?** Paper-first advice, techniques in order of usefulness, three projects that look good without drawing skill and a plain-language note on pigment safety.
9. **Which other articles should we link?** creative-hobbies-you-can-start-at-home, creative-hobby-starter-kits-worth-considering, how-to-start-junk-journaling.
10. **How does it contribute to long-term revenue?** Evergreen with solid affiliate fit (paper, sets, sketchbooks).
