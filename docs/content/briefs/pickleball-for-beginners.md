# Research brief: pickleball-for-beginners

Working title: Everything You Need for Your Pickleball Era
Status: reasoned draft brief. No live research has been done; nothing below is data.
Headline direction from the owner: 'Everything You Need for Your Pickleball Era'. Court dimensions, net heights and scoring remain in coworkChecks.

## Why would someone search this?
She has been invited to play or keeps seeing it everywhere, and wants to know what to buy, what the rules are and how to join without embarrassment. Commercial intent is strong ('pickleball paddle set', 'what do I need').

## Why would someone save it on Pinterest?
A starter checklist and a 'what to bring' graphic are natural pins, and the headline direction (hobby-era framing) matches the playful, aspirational tone. Evidence of save behavior is PENDING.

## Problem and aspiration
Problem: unfamiliar rules and jargon (the kitchen), uncertainty about equipment and the worry of joining strangers. Aspiration: being part of a friendly, social routine.

## Products that would genuinely help
Kinds only: a beginner paddle or paddle set, balls suited to indoor or outdoor play, court shoes, a visor, a tote or small bag, a water bottle.

## What she would reasonably need to buy
A paddle (or a set for two), balls and court-appropriate shoes if she lacks them.

## What she might already own
Trainers, a water bottle, a tote and sun protection.

## Optional products that help
A visor, a bag for kit, and later a better paddle once she knows her preference.

## What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results
Plain-English rules hedged where they could be wrong, an honest borrow-first path, and a clear separation of essentials from upgrades. Verified product picks later would explain what each helps with, rather than listing every paddle available.

## How this contributes to long-term revenue
High commercial intent with a clear beginner purchase (a paddle set), repeat purchases (balls) and a later upgrade path. Fits a pickleball collection and a shopping block. No earnings are claimed; viability is to be tested after publishing.
