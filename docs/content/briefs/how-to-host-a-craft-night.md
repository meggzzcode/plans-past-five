# Research brief: How to Host a Craft Night With Friends

Slug: how-to-host-a-craft-night
Status: written from editorial reasoning only. No search, traffic or price data has been collected; every data section below is pending Cowork.

## Why would someone search this?
She wants a social plan that is easy to host and not centered on alcohol, and does not know what to buy or how to run it.

## Why would someone save it on Pinterest?
Craft setups are visual and gift-adjacent; checklists and menus are classic saves.

## Problem and aspiration
Problem: planning a hostable, forgiving group activity. Aspiration: a creative, relaxed evening with friends.

## Products that would genuinely help
Friendship-bracelet kits, group painting kits, acrylic paints, cookie decorating kits, air-dry clay.

## What would she reasonably need to buy?
One craft's supplies per guest, table cover, wipes and a trash bag.

## What might she already own?
Table, tablecloth or newspaper, scissors, mugs, snacks, a playlist.

## Optional products that help
Aprons, trays, a separate snack table, a camera.

## What makes our recommendations better than generic shopping results
Timelines, per-guest budget ranges stated as typical, safety notes for glue guns, paint and clay, and a test-run tip. Products are described by what they help her do, claims are hedged and checked, and nothing is recommended unless it is verified.
