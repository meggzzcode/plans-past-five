# Research brief: how-to-host-a-paint-night-at-home

Status: draft. Answers below come from reasoning only. No data has been collected; every research section is pending.

1. **Why would someone search this?** Someone wants a social evening that is not drinks and searches for paint-and-sip at home ideas and supplies.
2. **Why would someone save it on Pinterest?** Table setups and canvas line-ups are visual and saveable, with peaks around birthdays and holidays.
3. **What problem or aspiration does it serve?** Problem: hosting a group activity without a studio or art skills. Aspiration: a relaxed evening with friends and something to take home.
4. **What products would genuinely help?** Group painting kits, an acrylic set, fine brushes and conversation cards, with canvas panels as a cheap base.
5. **What would she reasonably need to buy?** Canvases, paint and brushes. Typically about $10 to $20 per guest, to be verified.
6. **What might she already own?** Old towels, plates, jars, speaker, table.
7. **What optional products help?** Group kit, easel, conversation cards, playlist.
8. **What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?** A timeline, a single follow-along painting, set-up safety notes and handling for the friend who thinks she cannot paint.
9. **Which other articles should we link?** how-to-host-a-craft-night, girls-night-ideas-that-arent-drinks, how-to-start-air-dry-clay, watercolor-painting-for-beginners.
10. **How does it contribute to long-term revenue?** Evergreen with strong gifting and party seasons. Group kits are a natural product-pass target.
