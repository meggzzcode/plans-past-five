# Research brief: how-to-start-a-reading-journal

Status: draft. Answers below come from reasoning only. No data has been collected; every research section is pending.

1. **Why would someone search this?** Readers who forget what they read search for what to write and how to keep a journal going.
2. **Why would someone save it on Pinterest?** Layouts such as shelf spreads, tallies and wish lists are widely saved, with a January peak.
3. **What problem or aspiration does it serve?** Problem: forgetting books and abandoning journals. Aspiration: a lovely personal record of reading.
4. **What products would genuinely help?** A reading journal, pocket notebook, book light and magnetic bookmarks, each explained by its job.
5. **What would she reasonably need to buy?** Nothing necessarily. Typically up to about $20, to be verified.
6. **What might she already own?** Notebook, pen, sticky notes.
7. **What optional products help?** Purpose-made journal, book light, bookmarks, tape and stickers.
8. **What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?** A five-line template, three layouts, restart advice for abandoned habits and no claims about memory or wellbeing.
9. **Which other articles should we link?** how-to-start-a-book-club, how-to-start-junk-journaling, how-to-take-yourself-on-a-solo-coffee-shop-date, cozy-solo-dates-at-home.
10. **How does it contribute to long-term revenue?** Evergreen with a January peak, good affiliate fit for notebooks and book accessories.
