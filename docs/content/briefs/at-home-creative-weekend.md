# Research brief: A Complete Guide to Planning an At-Home Creative Weekend

Slug: `at-home-creative-weekend`. Status: draft. Written from reasoning only; no external data has been gathered.

## Why would someone search this?
She wants a staycation that feels like something, usually on a tight budget or after a heavy week. 'At home weekend ideas' and 'craft weekend' are planning queries from someone ready to gather supplies.

## Why would someone save it on Pinterest?
A two-day itinerary with a supply list is perfect for pins: cozy imagery, a schedule graphic and a supplies grid. It is saved before a planned weekend and reused seasonally.

## What problem or aspiration does it serve?
Problem: weekends at home that end in cleaning or couch-sinking. Aspiration: a weekend she can show for, with rest built in.

## What products would genuinely help?
Air-dry clay, a small set of acrylics and brushes, a small watercolor set and paper, a junk journal or notebook, washi tape, an apron.

## What would she reasonably need to buy?
Clay, a few acrylics, a small watercolor set, paper. Total typically in the region of a modest budget; verify before publication.

## What might she already own?
Notebook, scissors, glue stick, jar, paper towels, newspaper, rolling pin, a table.

## What optional products help?
Washi tape, stickers, apron, clay sealer.

## What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?
A real schedule with rest and a break from the craft zone, supply splits (essentials, own, optional, later), and troubleshooting. Generic results list kits; we show how the kit gets used across two days.
