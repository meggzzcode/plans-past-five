## Problem and aspiration
Problem: starting and sustaining a club. Aspiration: a regular, low-pressure tradition with friends.

## Products that would genuinely help
Conversation cards, book lights, reading journals, magnetic bookmarks.

## What would she reasonably need to buy?
Books, which can be borrowed from libraries, and somewhere to meet.

## What might she already own?
Books, a sofa, snacks, a group chat.

## Optional products that help
A reading journal, a book light, conversation cards, a bookmark.

## What makes our recommendations better than generic shopping results
A fair book-choice system, a plan for the month nobody finished, discussion prompts that name no real titles, and honest library notes. Products are described by what they help her do, claims are hedged and checked, and nothing is recommended unless it is verified.
