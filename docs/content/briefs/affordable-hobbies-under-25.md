# Research brief: 15 Affordable Hobbies You Can Start for Under $25

Slug: `affordable-hobbies-under-25`. Status: draft. Written from reasoning only; no external data has been gathered.

## Why would someone search this?
She wants to try something new without a big spend, often after seeing expensive-looking hobbies online. 'Cheap hobbies' and 'hobbies under $X' are budget-framed queries from someone ready to act.

## Why would someone save it on Pinterest?
Budget lists with a clear number in the title are highly saveable. A grid of small supplies (paints, floss, clay) is easy to illustrate, and 'start for under $25' reads as a promise she can screenshot.

## What problem or aspiration does it serve?
Problem: fear that a hobby is a financial commitment. Aspiration: a hobby that feels lavish in enjoyment and light in cost.

## What products would genuinely help?
Small watercolor set and paper, air-dry clay, embroidery starter kit, pocket notebook, small paint-by-numbers kit, embroidery floss for bracelets.

## What would she reasonably need to buy?
One small kit or set for the creative picks. For the free picks, nothing.

## What might she already own?
Notebook, pens, scissors, glue stick, bowl, tray, jars, comfortable shoes, tote.

## What optional products help?
Clip-on reading light, pencil case, binoculars (later), better brush (later).

## What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?
Honest framing: 'typically' ranges, an explicit explanation of why prices vary, a 'what to skip' note per hobby and a free-first ordering. Generic shopping results show the most clickable kit; we show the cheapest honest way in, including secondhand and borrowed.

## Which other articles should link to and from it?

## How does it contribute to long-term revenue?
A natural hub for budget starter kits, with many small-ticket product categories and a reason to return when she graduates to the next purchase. Strong internal-link source to individual how-to pages. No revenue figure is claimed.
