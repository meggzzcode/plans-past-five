# Research brief: how-to-start-crocheting

Status: draft. Answers below come from reasoning only. No data has been collected; every research section is pending.

1. **Why would someone search this?** A beginner wants to know what hook and yarn to buy and how to learn the first stitches.
2. **Why would someone save it on Pinterest?** Cozy yarn flat-lays, stitch guides and first-project ideas get saved, with seasonal peaks in autumn and winter.
3. **What problem or aspiration does it serve?** Problem: pattern jargon and yarn choice. Aspiration: making a small, useful, handmade thing.
4. **What products would genuinely help?** A mid-size hook, smooth light worsted yarn, a yarn needle and stitch markers. No catalog products exist yet.
5. **What would she reasonably need to buy?** A hook and a couple of balls of yarn. Typically about $10 to $25, to be verified.
6. **What might she already own?** Scissors, tape measure, a bag.
7. **What optional products help?** Stitch markers, a second hook size, a project tote.
8. **What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?** Forgiving-supply advice, a first project with a stitch count habit, US and UK terminology warning and pattern-reading help.
9. **Which other articles should we link?** how-to-start-embroidery, creative-hobbies-you-can-start-at-home, creative-hobby-starter-kits-worth-considering.
10. **How does it contribute to long-term revenue?** Evergreen with strong autumn seasonality. A verified-product pass should add hook and yarn types first.
