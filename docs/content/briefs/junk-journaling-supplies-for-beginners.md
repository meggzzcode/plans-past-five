# Research brief: junk-journaling-supplies-for-beginners

Status: draft. Answers below come from reasoning only. No data has been collected; every research section is pending.

1. **Why would someone search this?** A beginner who has seen junk journals on social media and wants to know what to buy, what she already has and what is a waste. The query is shopping-shaped and often arrives with a budget worry.
2. **Why would someone save it on Pinterest?** A tidy supplies flat-lay and a 'what you actually need' checklist are natural save-for-later content. People save it when planning a first craft haul.
3. **What problem or aspiration does it serve?** Problem: too many pretty products and no idea where to start. Aspiration: a charming, personal book made from scraps, without a costly craft-room.
4. **What products would genuinely help?** Thick-paged journals, washi tape, one cohesive sticker set, fine pens, glue sticks and a bone folder, each explained by the step it makes easier.
5. **What would she reasonably need to buy?** A thick-paged notebook, glue stick, tape and a pen. Typically low double digits at most.
6. **What might she already own?** Scissors, ruler, glue, envelopes, receipts, tickets, tags, cardboard, ribbon and string.
7. **What optional products help?** Stickers, stamps, binder clips, double-sided tape, paper packs, punches, wax seals.
8. **What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?** A sorted free-first list that says what to skip, a palette-first buying rule, a realistic budget with tiers, and later product pass limited to verified items so every recommendation has a reason.
9. **Which other articles should we link?** how-to-start-junk-journaling, creative-hobbies-you-can-start-at-home, creative-hobby-starter-kits-worth-considering, hobby-era-tracker printable.
10. **How does it contribute to long-term revenue?** Evergreen shopping-intent content with high affiliate fit (low-cost, repeat-buy consumables). Supports a future junk journal kit and printable pack.
