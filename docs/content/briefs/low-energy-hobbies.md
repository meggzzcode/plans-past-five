# Research brief: 20 Cozy Hobbies to Try After a Long Day

Slug: `low-energy-hobbies`. Status: draft. Written from reasoning only; no external data has been gathered.

## Why would someone search this?
Someone home from work, flat and a bit guilty about scrolling, wants an idea that needs no energy, no skill and no shopping trip. Phrases like 'cozy hobbies', 'relaxing hobbies after work' and 'hobbies when tired' describe a mood as much as a topic, so she arrives wanting permission as well as a list.

## Why would someone save it on Pinterest?
'Cozy' is highly visual and saveable: a blanket, a lamp, yarn, a puzzle on a tray. A numbered list she can screenshot into a board titled something like 'Quiet evenings' is the natural behaviour. She saves it for a future tired Tuesday, not for today.

## What problem or aspiration does it serve?
Problem: evenings lost to autopilot scrolling. Aspiration: an evening that feels chosen and soft, without turning rest into another project.

## What products would genuinely help?
Beginner craft kits (embroidery, paint by numbers, small watercolor set), a clip-on or small reading light, a reading journal, a lap tray, a basket to keep a project by the sofa.

## What would she reasonably need to buy?
Almost nothing. At most one small kit or one skein of yarn with a hook or needles, bought in the smallest size.

## What might she already own?
Pens, notebook, blanket, lamp, glue, scrap paper, magazines, a mug, a library card.

## What optional products help?
Reading light, lap tray, project basket, reading journal, a nicer blanket.

## What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?
Hobbies sorted by effort rather than by category, a two-minute-setup rule, honest 'stop whenever' framing, and a 'shrink it' section for days when even this is too much. Generic shopping results list kits; we say which one to open tonight and why it is forgiving.

## Which other articles should link to and from it?

## How does it contribute to long-term revenue?
Evergreen, high-intent for starter craft kits and cozy-evening accessories (lights, journals, blankets). Likely to resurface every autumn and winter, and a natural hub that feeds the craft how-tos. Revenue is long-tail affiliate on small-ticket items plus newsletter growth; no income claim is made.
