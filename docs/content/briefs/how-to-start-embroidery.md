# Research brief: how-to-start-embroidery

Status: draft. Answers below come from reasoning only. No data has been collected; every research section is pending.

1. **Why would someone search this?** Someone wants a portable, quiet craft and searches for stitches and what to buy.
2. **Why would someone save it on Pinterest?** Hoop close-ups, stitch cheat sheets and small floral designs are classic save content.
3. **What problem or aspiration does it serve?** Problem: unsure about stitches and supplies. Aspiration: stitching something she is proud to hang.
4. **What products would genuinely help?** Hoop, six-strand floss, embroidery needles, even-weave fabric, small scissors and a beginner kit.
5. **What would she reasonably need to buy?** A hoop, floss, needles and fabric. Typically about $10 to $30, to be verified.
6. **What might she already own?** Scissors, pencil, scrap cotton.
7. **What optional products help?** Water-soluble pen, needle minder, thread organizer, pattern book.
8. **What would make our recommendations better than generic shopping results?** A stitch-first route on scrap fabric, kit-or-loose guidance and fixes for tangles and puckering.
9. **Which other articles should we link?** how-to-start-crocheting, creative-hobbies-you-can-start-at-home, creative-hobby-starter-kits-worth-considering.
10. **How does it contribute to long-term revenue?** Evergreen. Kits convert well for gifts.
