Twelve project ideas

Beginner-friendly projects that look good even if you've never crafted before.

something for everyone

1. Bracelets

Beads and elastic. Quick and wearable.

2. Air-dry clay dishes

Roll, stamp and cut. Dry overnight.

3. Painted tote bags

Fabric paint and stencils from cardboard.

4. Junk journal pages

Scraps, glue and washi tape.

5. Pressed-flower cards

Press ahead of time, then glue on cards.

6. Candle labels

Design labels for jars.

7. Embroidery hoops

Simple stitches on a scrap of fabric.

8. Collage postcards

Magazine cut-outs in themed palettes.

9. Friendship zines

Fold, cut and fill with doodles.

10. Painted rocks

Smooth stones and acrylic pens.

11. Paper flowers

Tissue or crepe paper and wire.

12. Custom stationery

Stamps, pens and plain cards.
