The seven-day countdown

Spread the work across the week so the day itself is calm.

calm hosts plan early

7 days out
  • Pick the date and the theme
  • Send invites
  • Sketch the menu
  • Check dietary needs
5 days out
  • Plan the activity or game
  • Order anything online
  • Pick the playlist
  • Borrow chairs and extra plates
3 days out
  • Confirm guest count
  • Make a shopping list
  • Clear the surfaces
  • Plan the table
2 days out
  • Shop for food and drink
  • Buy flowers or candles
  • Prep a make-ahead dish
  • Charge speakers
1 day out
  • Clean main rooms and bathroom
  • Chill drinks
  • Set the table
  • Send a reminder text
The day
  • Put food in the oven early
  • Light candles at arrival time
  • Start the music
  • Sit down and enjoy

Game or movie night

Keep it cozy and flexible.

pick three

Game night
  • Pick three games: calm, medium, silly
  • Print simple rules
  • Pens and paper
  • Snacks that don't make fingers sticky
  • A tiny trophy for the winner
Movie night
  • Pick the film (or a short list)
  • Check the screen and sound
  • Blankets and cushions
  • Popcorn and sweet snacks
  • Pause breaks planned

After-party reset

Ten minutes the next morning turns a messy home back into a calm one.

10 minutes tomorrow

Reset checklist
  • Open the windows
  • Collect glasses and plates
  • Run the dishwasher or wash by hand
  • Wipe counters and the table
  • Take out bins
  • Return borrowed items
  • Put leftovers away
  • Text a thank-you to the guests
  • Write down what worked
