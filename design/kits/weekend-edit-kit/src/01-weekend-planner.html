How to plan a weekend

Spend ten minutes on Thursday evening. Pick one anchor plan, one rest block and one thing to look forward to.

ten minutes on thursday

  1. Choose a theme. Cozy, social, outdoorsy or creative.
  2. Pick one anchor plan per day. Everything else stays flexible.
  3. Protect a rest block. Put it in writing.
  4. Check money and weather, then pick a backup.
  5. Tick it off and write one memory.
The anchor rule: one planned thing per day is enough. More than two and a weekend becomes a to-do list.
