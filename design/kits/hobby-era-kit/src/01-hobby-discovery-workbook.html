How to use this workbook

Work through it in order over a week or two, or jump to the page you need. Print once, write in pencil, reprint freely for personal use.

start here

  1. Audit your life. Time, energy, money and space, honestly.
  2. Map your cravings. What do you want a hobby to give you?
  3. Browse and rate the menu. Thirty-six ideas, one score each.
  4. Design experiments. Six small trials with a plan, a cap and a date.
  5. Score what happened and decide: keep, pause or swap.
  6. Write your hobby era plan. A season-long plan you'll actually follow.
Pencil tip: rate things quickly. First instincts are more useful than careful ones.
