Professionally off duty.

How to use this journal

No rules, no streaks and no wrong answers. It's a place to notice what you like.

write badly

  1. Pick a page that matches how you feel.
  2. Write for ten minutes, or three lines.
  3. Date it and tick the mood dots.
  4. Flip back after a month and look for patterns.
Write badly. This journal is not for sharing. Spelling, neatness and grammar are all optional.
My writing ritualsTea, a candle, a favorite pen, a certain chair…
If you get stuck, try one of these starters
  • Today I noticed…
  • I'd like to try…
  • Something that made me smile was…
  • I'm proud that…
  • Next week I want to…
