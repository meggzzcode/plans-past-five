Run of show

A gentle timeline so nothing sneaks up on you. Tick it off as you go.

enjoy your own party

A few days before
  • Send invites and track replies
  • Pick the playlist
  • Check supplies and order what's missing
  • Plan seating (floor cushions count)
The day before
  • Confirm numbers and send a reminder
  • Shop for food and drink
  • Prep anything that can be made ahead
  • Wash glasses, plates and napkins
Two hours before
  • Tidy the main room and a bathroom
  • Set out the activity
  • Light candles, start the playlist
  • Food out of the fridge
Thirty minutes before
  • Get dressed, get a drink
  • Fill water jugs and ice
  • Phone on silent
  • Take a deep breath

Twelve easy themes

Pick one, copy its idea and adapt freely. Each can be done on a small budget with things you mostly own.

borrow any of these

1. Bracelet bar

A bowl of beads, some cord and a playlist. Everyone leaves with one for themselves and one to give.

2. Mahjong and snacks

Teach the basics, share a snack spread and keep the rules loose enough to laugh.

3. Paint-and-sip (tea optional)

Pre-sketched canvases, an easy subject and a prize for the funniest result.

4. Junk journal swap

Everyone brings scraps and glue. Trade, make a page and swap one at the end.

5. Cook-along

One recipe, one table, everyone cooks a course. Pass the plates and eat at once.

6. Clothes swap

Bring five things you're done with, take five you love. Mirror and music essential.

7. Book club dinner

Cozy food, one question each and the freedom to ignore the book entirely.

8. Movie and manicure

Cheesy film, nail polish and bowls of popcorn. Zero planning needed.

9. Game night

Three games ranging from calm to chaos and a winner's tiny trophy.

10. Picnic in the park

Blankets, a shared spread and a camera for golden hour.

11. Craft supply swap

Everyone empties the craft closet, trades and ends up with something new.

12. Garden night

Cuttings, small pots and a plant swap. Take home something alive.
