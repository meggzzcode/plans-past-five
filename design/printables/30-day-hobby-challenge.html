How it works

Pick one hobby (or a sampler) and do something tiny with it every day for a month. Fifteen minutes absolutely counts.

fifteen minutes counts

  1. Pick your hobby. One you're curious about, or “a new thing each week”.
  2. Set your start date and put a 15-minute slot in your calendar.
  3. Follow the daily prompt on the next pages. Tick the box. That's it.
  4. Miss a day? Carry on from where you are. No streak police here.
Seven-day rhythm: Week 1 is Show up, week 2 is Play, week 3 is Stretch, week 4 is Own it. Every seventh day is a rest day for noticing.

Easy-mode rules

Five minutes beats zero. If a day is rough, do the smallest version.

Spend almost nothing. Use what you have for all thirty days.
