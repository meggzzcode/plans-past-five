How this workbook works

Six small steps from “I should have a hobby” to “this is my thing”. Do one a night, or all in a Sunday afternoon.

no experience needed

  1. Check your real life. How much time, energy and money do you really have? (Page 3)
  2. Name the craving. Calm? Company? Making something with your hands? (Page 4)
  3. Browse the menu. Circle what makes you curious, no overthinking. (Page 5)
  4. Shortlist three. Plan one tiny, cheap first try for each. (Page 6)
  5. Track what you try. Rate how it felt, not how it looked. (Page 7)
  6. Decide. Keep it, pause it or swap it for something else. (Page 8)
The only rules: try each hobby at least twice before you decide, keep the first session under two hours, and spend as close to nothing as you can.
