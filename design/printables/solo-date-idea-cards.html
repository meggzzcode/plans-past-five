How to use these cards

Cut them out, fold them or leave them flat, drop them in a jar and pull one when the weekend feels empty.

party of one

  1. Print pages 3 to 6 on US Letter. Thicker paper makes them last, but plain works fine.
  2. Cut along the dashed lines. Each page makes six cards.
  3. Shuffle and pull one. Veto allowed once, then you're committed.
  4. Put it in the calendar before you get home. A date you don't schedule is just a nice idea.
  5. Tick it off on the card when you're done and keep the pile.
Make it feel like a date: dress for the version of you you'd like to meet, put your phone on airplane mode and show up a few minutes early.
Cost labels

Free: costs nothing or just a bus fare.

Low spend: a coffee, a ticket, a snack.

Treat yourself: a proper meal or class. Set your own cap.

Solo-date sense
  • Tell a friend where you're going and when you'll be back.
  • Choose daytime or busy places if you prefer.
  • Leave when you're done. You don't owe anyone a full evening.
